BOPEU

http://bopeu.org.bw/

The **Botswana Public Employees Union (BOPEU)** is one of the largest and most influential trade unions in Botswana. It represents a wide range of workers, primarily from the public service, but also from parastatals and organizations where the government has a financial interest.

Here is a comprehensive breakdown of everything you need to know about BOPEU:

---

## 1. History and Evolution
* **Origins:** BOPEU’s roots go back to **1942** with the Bechuanaland Protectorate associations. In 1960, these merged to form the **Botswana Civil Servants Association (BCSA)**.
* **Transformation:** In **2007**, the BCSA officially transformed into a trade union, rebranding as BOPEU. This shift allowed it to engage in formal collective bargaining and legal labor representation.
* **Legal Status:** It is duly registered under the Trade Unions and Employers’ Organization Act of Botswana.

## 2. Membership and Scope
* **Who can join?** Membership includes government employees, local authorities, and staff from organizations like:
    * Botswana Unified Revenue Services (BURS)
    * Local Enterprise Authority (LEA)
    * Civil Aviation Authority of Botswana (CAAB)
    * Botswana Examinations Council (BEC)
* **Size:** As of 2023, the union reported over **37,000 members**.

## 3. Core Services and Mandate
BOPEU acts as a bargaining agent to negotiate:
* **Salary & Benefits:** Negotiating increments, allowances, and improved working conditions.
* **Labour Representation:** Providing legal support and representation during disciplinary hearings, grievances, and disputes (up to the Industrial Court).
* **Training:** Equipping "shop stewards" (elected member representatives) with skills in labor relations and mediation.

## 4. The "Babereki" Business Wing
One of BOPEU's unique features is its highly successful investment arm, **Babereki Investments**, which provides commercial services to members:
* **Babereki Insurance Brokers (BIB):** Offers affordable funeral schemes, life assurance (GLA), and motor insurance. A major recent update (2023) saw funeral cover increase from P30,000 to **P60,000**.
* **Babereki SACCOS:** A Savings and Credit Co-operative Society that allows members to save and access loans at competitive rates.
* **Housing (BOPEU Enclave):** The union recently launched a **P125 million** housing project in Mmopane to provide affordable two-bedroom apartments for its members.

## 5. Current Leadership & Structure
* **President:** **Masego Mogwera** (who made history as one of the first women to lead a major union in Botswana).
* **Structure:** The union is governed by a **National Executive Committee (NEC)** and operates through 31 districts across Botswana. It also features specialized councils, such as the Women’s Council and Youth Council.
