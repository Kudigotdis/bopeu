BOPEU Staff Info:

To adapt the database structure for BOPEU Staff users while integrating your new data requirements (WhatsApp, mobile wallet accounts, residing addresses, and social media handles), we need to split this into two parts.
First, we must establish what Personal Identifiable Information (PII) to collect from the Staff themselves to secure the internal backend. Second, we must format the new fields you requested so they function cleanly within both member and staff schemas in Botswana's unique digital ecosystem.

Part 1: What You Missed for the Staff Profile Schema

Just like members, BOPEU staff cannot simply have an email and password. To prevent internal fraud, trace data leaks, and comply with the Botswana Data Protection Act (DPA), their system profiles must capture the following foundational information:
• Omang Number (National ID): Required to legally tie system actions to a real person. If a staff member downloads a member list illicitly, the system audit log must tie that action back to their legal identity, not just a generic username.
• Official Corporate Email Address: Used exclusively for password resets, and system alerts (e.g., name@bopeu.org.bw or name@babereki.co.bw).
• Employee/Staff ID Number: The primary database key used to map their profile to human resources and payroll tables.
• Department / Cost Centre Code: Maps the staff user to their structural unit (e.g., HQ-Marketing, Francistown-Sales, Gaborone-Finance).
• Line Manager / Approver ID: A relational database link defining who has the authority to approve data overrides or document uploads committed by this staff user.
• Active IP / Device Whitelist Bindings: To ensure secure access, the database should track the physical MAC address or authorized IP range of the staff member's work laptop or official field tablet, blocking access from unsecured networks.

Part 2: Structuring Your New Requested Fields

When integrating WhatsApp, network banking, addresses, and social handles, they need to be broken down into structured database fields to match Botswana's local telecommunications layout (Mascom, Orange, BTC).

📱 Mobile Network & Number Data

Do not use a single generic text field for mobile numbers. You must break them down into specific relational tables to account for users who swap SIMs or use multiple networks:
• Primary Contact Number: The phone number used for system SMS alerts.
• Mobile Network Provider (Dropdown/Enum): Must restrict inputs to MASCOM, ORANGE, or BTC. This flag is vital for the Marketing team when auditing closed-user-group (CUG) setups or target campaigns.
• WhatsApp Number Flag (Boolean): A true/false toggle tied to the number. This tells the system's communication engine whether to send a notification via standard SMS or route it through an automated WhatsApp Business API gateway (which is often cheaper for mass union broadcasts).

🏦 Mobile Network Banking (Mobile Money/Wallets)

In Botswana, mobile banking heavily relies on Mobile Money (MNO) wallets like MyZaka (Mascom), Orange Money (Orange), and Smega (BTC) alongside traditional commercial banks. The database needs to capture:
• Mobile Wallet Provider (Enum): Restrict inputs to MYZAKA, ORANGE_MONEY, SMEGA, or TRADITIONAL_BANK.
• Mobile Wallet Account Number: The mobile number registered to that wallet.
• Linked Commercial Bank Name & Account Number: For back-office roles like ROLE_FINANCE_AUDITOR or ROLE_WELFARE_CLAIMS. When a member requests a fast-tracked Babereki loan or a funeral claim payout through the app, the system needs these structured fields to execute automated payouts directly via electronic funds transfer (EFT) or mobile wallet push APIs.

📍 Residing Address Data

To segment members and field sales staff effectively, split the address structure into clear administrative tiers rather than a single text block:
• Residing Town / City (Enum): Standardized local categories (e.g., Gaborone, Francistown, Palapye, Maun, Kanye, Mochudi).
• Residing Area / Neighbourhood (Text): Specific locations within those towns (e.g., Phase 2, Block 8, Gaborone West, Gerald Estates, Central).
• Physical Plot Number (Text/Alphanumeric): Essential for Botswana addresses (e.g., Plot 54352) and directly required by Babereki Insurance Brokers when writing short-term household contents insurance policies.

🌐 Social Media Handles

Collecting social media coordinates helps the Marketing team verify user-submitted app content and execute targeted social media advocacy campaigns:
• Facebook Profile URL / Handle: Essential for matching members to activities on the highly active BOPEU official Facebook ecosystem.
• Twitter/X Handle (@handle): For tracking public-sector union conversations and labor trends.
• Instagram Handle & TikTok Handle: Primarily used to identify and empower younger union members or local field shop stewards to act as organic brand ambassadors for recruitment campaigns.
