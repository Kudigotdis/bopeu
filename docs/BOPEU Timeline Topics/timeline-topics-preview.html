Our Values

What We Stand For

Justice. Equality. Representation. Solidarity. Empowerment.

BOPEU works to protect workers, strengthen their collective voice and promote fairer workplaces and better conditions of service across Botswana.

Read full topic

A fair workplace does not happen by accident. It takes informed workers, effective representation, constructive engagement and the willingness to stand together. BOPEU helps members understand workplace processes, raise concerns through appropriate channels and participate in collective efforts to improve conditions of service.

Effective labour representation

BOPEU represents members’ workplace interests through appropriate union structures and processes. Depending on the issue and applicable rules, support may include advice, engagement with an employer, assistance during a grievance or disciplinary process, and representation in formal labour forums.

Protecting workers’ rights

Members should be treated fairly and consistently, with workplace procedures handled according to applicable laws, policies, collective agreements and conditions of service. Keep records and seek guidance early if you believe a process is unfair.

Fair wages and better working conditions

BOPEU advocates for fair remuneration, transparent application of employment conditions, safe workplaces and improvements to conditions of service. Salary adjustments, allowances and other changes depend on negotiations, applicable agreements, employer decisions and formal implementation; distinguish proposals from approved outcomes.

Social justice, equality and good governance

A fair public service values dignity, equal treatment, accountability and access to fair processes. Members should be able to raise concerns without discrimination and understand how workplace decisions are made.

Empowered and informed members

Read verified notices, access approved documents, attend branch activities and ask questions through the appropriate shop steward or official contact channel.

Worker solidarity

Solidarity means supporting one another respectfully across ministries, districts, occupations and generations. Share verified information and participate constructively in union activities.

What BOPEU does

BOPEU’s work may include labour representation, member organising, dispute-resolution guidance, collective bargaining, grievance support and assistance during disciplinary hearings, subject to union procedures, applicable law and the circumstances of each case.

What members can do

  • Read official notices and distinguish confirmed decisions from proposals.
  • Keep appointment letters, payslips and relevant correspondence for workplace matters.
  • Contact the relevant shop steward, regional office or BOPEU channel for case-specific guidance.
  • Do not post confidential personnel records or another member’s personal information publicly.

Member Benefits — What You Get

Your membership card connects you to more.

Explore services, schemes and partner offers that may be available to eligible members, from insurance and savings to health, lifestyle and family support.

Read full topic

BOPEU membership can connect members with union services, Babereki-related financial and insurance services, and participating partner offers. The app is a place to discover current offers, read their terms and learn how to apply or verify eligibility.

Your digital membership card

Your card supports membership identification in approved app and partner workflows. It does not automatically guarantee every discount, loan, insurance benefit or service. Eligibility, good-standing status, partner participation and offer terms may apply.

Financial and savings services

Members may be able to explore savings and credit services through Babereki SACCOS or other approved arrangements. Before accepting credit, review interest and fees, repayment frequency, total repayment, required documents and consequences of missed payments.

Insurance and family protection

The app may provide information about funeral cover, group life assurance and other insurance products. Covered persons, benefit amounts, exclusions, waiting periods, premiums and claim requirements are determined by current policy wording and insurer decisions.

Health and wellness offers

Confirm the provider branch, covered service, discount calculation, exclusions and verification process before booking or paying. A discount is not a substitute for medical advice or medical-aid authorisation where required.

Shopping, travel and lifestyle offers

Offers may be limited to selected locations, products, dates or payment methods. Check the individual offer card for validity dates, proof-of-membership requirements and terms.

Educational and family support

Where an assistance programme is open, the app should explain eligibility, eligible expenses, required documents, application windows, assessment criteria and how decisions are communicated. Published maximums are not guaranteed awards.

What members can do

  • Open the specific benefit card and read its eligibility criteria and full terms.
  • Check offer dates and whether it applies at your intended branch.
  • Use only official application and partner contacts shown in the verified app.
  • Keep receipts, application references and written confirmations.
  • Report outdated partner details through official BOPEU channels.

Complete Family Protection

Because your family deserves financial security when it matters most.

Learn how member funeral cover is intended to support eligible families, what to verify in the policy and how to prepare for a claim.

Read full topic

Bereavement can bring emotional strain alongside urgent practical and financial responsibilities. Funeral cover is designed to help eligible beneficiaries manage some costs associated with a death. Keep policy information and family details up to date.

Who may be covered?

The supplied programme description refers to members, spouses and children under 21. The exact definition of an eligible spouse or child, age limits, dependency requirements, cover start date and extended-family options must be checked against the current policy schedule.

Understand the cover

Check the insured benefit amount, premium or payroll deduction, covered lives, exclusions, waiting periods, policy status and claim process. A headline benefit amount may not apply to every member or circumstance.

Keep family information current

Update spouse and dependent details where required and confirm that names, identity information and relationships match supporting records. Submit sensitive documents only through approved secure channels—not a public timeline or unverified WhatsApp number.

When a death occurs

Contact the official BOPEU/Babereki claims channel promptly. Ask for the current claim form, notification and submission deadlines, document checklist, claimant requirements and claim reference.

Documents commonly requested

Depending on policy and circumstances, a claim may require a completed form, official death certificate or accepted proof of death, identity documents for the deceased and claimant, proof of relationship or dependency, policy details and payment instructions. Follow the insurer’s current checklist.

Track the claim

Record when the claim was reported, documents submitted, receiving office and claim reference. If a document is unavailable or a deadline is at risk, contact the claims team immediately and ask for written guidance.

What members can do

  • Confirm who is listed as covered in the current policy.
  • Keep official claims contacts available to your family.
  • Ask for the latest approved claim deadlines and document checklist.
  • Submit documents through verified secure channels and retain a receipt or reference.

Financial Wellbeing

Financial Support When Life Takes an Unexpected Turn

Savings, credit and assistance for life’s difficult moments.

Find out about available savings, credit and assistance routes, and compare the full cost and repayment obligations before taking a loan.

Read full topic

Unexpected expenses such as urgent travel, essential repairs or a family emergency can pressure a household budget. BOPEU-related financial services may provide options for eligible members, but every product has its own assessment process, terms and limits. Borrow only after understanding the full cost.

Babereki SACCOS savings and credit

A savings and credit cooperative can help members build a savings habit and may provide credit under its rules. Ask about membership, minimum savings, withdrawal conditions, loan eligibility, interest or charges, guarantors and repayment terms.

Emergency lending

Confirm current loan amounts, eligibility, required documents, interest rate and fees, repayment period, repayment method and whether approval times are estimates or service commitments. A stated turnaround is not a guarantee of approval or disbursement.

Affordability before applying

Compare instalments with take-home pay after essential expenses and existing deductions. Ask for the total amount repayable, not only the monthly instalment. Consider whether repayments remain manageable if overtime or variable income changes.

Avoid unverified offers and scams

Use contact details published in the verified BOPEU app or official channels. Do not pay unofficial release fees or share a PIN, password or security code.

If you already have a loan

Check your statement and payslip for deductions, record discrepancies and contact the provider through an official channel. Ask for a written balance and repayment schedule; seek help early if repayment becomes difficult.

What members can do

  • Read current product terms and eligibility criteria.
  • Request a written quotation or repayment schedule before accepting credit.
  • Keep your application reference and official receipts.
  • Never share mobile-money PINs, banking passwords or security codes.

Support your studies, skills and long-term career development.

Learn how educational assistance may support eligible study costs, what evidence to prepare and how decisions are communicated.

Read full topic

Education and professional development can strengthen a member’s career and the public services communities rely on. Where BOPEU or a related scheme offers educational assistance, use the published application rules to understand eligibility, covered expenses and assessment processes.

What expenses might be considered?

Depending on the approved scheme, eligible costs may include tuition, required study materials, books, study accommodation or academic research. The scheme rules determine coverage; some personal or retrospective expenses may be excluded.

Who can apply?

Eligibility may depend on membership status, good standing, course or institution, academic progress, available funding and the purpose of the application. Check the current call for applications.

Prepare a complete application

Common evidence may include an application form, proof of admission or registration, fee statement or quotation, course details, academic records where required, and proof of identity or membership. Follow the current checklist and secure upload route.

Understand amount and conditions

A published maximum—such as P30,000 in the supplied draft description—is not an automatic award. Approved rules determine the maximum, expenses, assessment criteria, payment method and any reporting or repayment conditions.

Track your application

Save the submission confirmation and reference. Check whether the application is received, awaiting documents, under review, approved, declined or awaiting disbursement. Respond to requests by the stated deadline.

What members can do

  • Read the current bursary notice and confirm the application window.
  • Gather only documents requested in the official checklist.
  • Submit through the approved channel and save your reference.
  • Do not assume funding is approved until you receive formal written confirmation.

Loan amount and term in the supplied brief

Financial Wellbeing

Babereki SACCOS — Savings & Credit

Cooperative savings and credit solutions for BOPEU members.

Learn how cooperative savings and credit may support financial planning, and what to ask before joining or applying for credit.

Read full topic

Savings can create a buffer for planned expenses and unexpected events. A savings and credit cooperative may give eligible members a structured way to save and, subject to its rules, apply for credit. Understand the cooperative’s membership rules and product terms before making financial decisions.

Build a savings habit

Choose a realistic amount and ask how contributions are recorded, when savings can be withdrawn, whether notice is required, and whether any balance is restricted or pledged against a loan. Keep statements and check transactions regularly.

Understand membership

Ask about eligibility, joining requirements, member rights and responsibilities, share capital if applicable, account fees, governance, complaint routes and how to update personal details. Read current member rules and fee schedules.

Credit and loan eligibility

Access to credit is not automatic. It may depend on membership duration, savings history, affordability, guarantors, outstanding obligations and lending policy. Obtain a written offer with rate, fees, instalments, total repayment and consequences of missed payments.

Statements and reconciliation

Review your SACCOS statement and payroll deductions. If a payment is missing or a balance does not match, contact the official channel and provide date, amount and reference—never send your password or PIN.

Plan for long-term security

Savings work best with a budget and realistic goals. Keep emergency funds separate from money committed to loan repayments and avoid borrowing more than you can comfortably repay.

What members can do

  • Request the current SACCOS membership guide and fee schedule.
  • Set a savings target that fits your budget.
  • Read loan terms and ask for a written total repayment amount.
  • Check statements and report discrepancies through official channels.
