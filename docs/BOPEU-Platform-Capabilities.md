## Slide 16 — The funeral cover scheme

**Core cover — P 65,000** for the member and spouse.

**Subscription — 1% of basic salary**, floored at **P 50.00** and capped at **P 120.00**, collected by payroll stop-order.

**Children are included at no additional cost**, on age bands:

| Age band | Benefit |
|---|---|
| 16 – 21 years | P 65,000 |
| 7 – 15 years | P 32,500 |
| 0 – 6 years | P 16,250 |

**Optional Premium Waiver — +P 4.70** takes the compulsory premium from P 61.70 to P 66.40 and keeps dependants covered for 12 months if the main member passes on.

> **Speaker note:** "This is the scheme in one slide. The compulsory premium is a floating
> one percent of salary, floor and cap — so a P 5,500 basic salary pays P 55.00. Children
> cost the member nothing, which is the point of the scheme: a member on a modest salary
> still gets P 65,000 of cover for the family."

---

## Slide 17 — Funeral cover — optional rate tables

**Parents** — up to 4, entry to age 80:

| Cover | Premium | With waiver |
|---|---|---|
| P 10,000 | P 24.70 | P 26.50 |
| P 15,000 | P 37.00 | P 39.70 |
| P 20,000 | P 49.30 | P 53.10 |
| P 25,000 | P 61.70 | P 66.30 |
| P 40,000 | P 98.60 | P 106.00 |
| P 50,000 | P 123.30 | P 132.50 |

**Extended family** — up to 8, nominees under age 75 — 9 cover levels from P 5,000 to P 50,000, premiums from **P 17.10** to **P 170.20**. A member may nominate extended family only when within **5 years of attaining age 60**.

**Children over 21** — 3 levels: P 30,000 · P 40,000 · P 50,000 at **P 8.50 / P 11.30 / P 14.10** per child per month. Adult child cover ceases at 35 — the parent must move the member to extended family cover.

> **Speaker note:** "These are the optional layers. Parents and extended family are where
> members add cover incrementally — a member can start at ten thousand and step up. The
> extended family table is the one members ask about most, because it covers uncles, aunts,
> cousins and in-laws. Two conditions worth knowing: extended family is only available to a
> member within five years of sixty, and adult child cover stops at thirty-five, so members
> should convert to extended family rather than let it lapse."

## Slide 18 — Funeral cover — terms and waiting periods

**Who the scheme covers:** an employee of the Government of the Republic of Botswana, or of any organisation where government has a financial interest, who has not attained age 65. Group Life Assurance participation is **mandatory for all members**.

**Who counts as a child:** by birth, step, adopted, or born out of wedlock, under 21 — extended to **25 while in full study**. No age limit for a child with a proven physical or mental disability. A stillborn child of 24 weeks or more gestation is covered.

**Waiting periods:**

| Cover | Waiting period |
|---|---|
| Main member and family | 1 calendar month |
| Parents | 6 months (natural death) |
| Extended family | 12 months (natural death) |
| Suicide | 24 months |
| Accidental death | Immediate, once one month's premium is paid |
| New benefit after an amendment | 6 months |

**Cover ceases** on written termination of membership, on 3 consecutive months of unpaid premiums, or on the day the main member dies.

> **Speaker note:** "I want to be straight with you about the waiting periods, because it is
> the number one thing members complain about when a claim is declined. The main member and
> family are covered after one month. The long waits — six months for parents, twelve for
> extended family, twenty-four for suicide — are the underwriter's rules, not BOPEU's, and we
> publish them rather than let a family find out at claim stage. Note the exception that
> matters most in practice: accidental death is covered immediately, and that is the
> majority of claims we see."

## Slide 20 — Funeral claims

- **Guided 5-document checklist** — nothing is filed until all five are ready
  1. Death Certificate
  2. Claimant (Next of Kin) ID / Omang
  3. Claimant bank details
  4. Relationship proof — marriage or birth certificate
  5. Contact details for follow-up
- **Live progress bar** — "3 of 5 documents ready (60%)"
- **Submit** unlocks only when the checklist is complete
- One-tap hand-off to the union for submission
- Claims must be **notified within 6 months** of death; all documents within **12 months**
- **Paid within 48 hours** of the underwriter receiving all requested documents
- Submitted at the **Francistown, Kang, Palapye and Maun** branches; **Gaborone** and surrounding areas at Babereki Insurance Brokers Head Office

> **Speaker note:** "Claims are where a member is under most pressure, so this is the flow we
> made most defensive. The submit button is literally disabled until all five documents are
> ticked. The six-month and twelve-month windows are the two things members get wrong — the
> platform now states both."

## Appendix — Scheme reference

Figures per the BOPEU Membership Application Form. Underwritten by **Bona Life Insurance**. Premiums in Botswana Pula (BWP).

**Compulsory premium:** 1% of basic salary · floor P 50.00 · cap P 120.00
**Premium waiver:** +P 4.70 (P 61.70 → P 66.40), 12-month premium waiver
**Core cover:** P 65,000 (member and spouse)
**Children included at no cost:** 0–6 yrs P 16,250 · 7–15 yrs P 32,500 · 16–21 yrs P 65,000
**Maximums:** 6 children · 4 parents (entry to age 80) · 8 extended family (under 75)
**Extended family eligibility:** member within 5 years of attaining age 60
**Child definition:** under 21, extended to 25 while in full study · no age limit where a disability is proven · adult child cover ceases at 35
**Member eligibility:** GRB employee, or an organisation where government has a financial interest · under age 65
**GLA participation:** mandatory for all members

**Waiting periods:** 1 calendar month (main member and family) · 6 months (parents, natural death) · 12 months (extended family, natural death) · 24 months (suicide) · immediate for accidental death once one month's premium is paid · 6 months for any new benefit added by amendment

**Ceases:** written termination of membership · 3 consecutive months of unpaid premiums · the day the main member dies

**Cut-off:** 15th of every month; membership takes effect on the first successful deduction
**Claims:** notify within 6 months of death · all documents within 12 months · **paid within 48 hours** of receiving all requested documents
**Claims submitted at:** BOPEU branches — Francistown, Kang, Palapye, Maun · Gaborone and surrounding areas at Babereki Insurance Brokers Head Office

**Borrowing affordability notice:** borrowing more than you can afford can lead to severe
financial difficulties.
