What you get as a member

BOPEU membership unlocks financial, family, health, lifestyle and legal support.

🎓

Educational Bursaries

Interest-free assistance up to P30,000 for tuition, accommodation, books and research. Repay over 24 months.

🏠

Building Materials Scheme

Subsidised materials credit through Archein Hardware — up to P200,000 over 36 months. No deposit required.

💰

Emergency Loans

Quick P2,001 – P20,000 loans through Babereki SACCOS with a 48-hour approval turnaround.

🛡️

Funeral Cover

Group cover underwritten by Metropolitan Life — 1% of salary (capped P39.90 – P100). Includes 12-month premium waiver.

⚖️

Legal Representation

Free legal support on workplace disputes, disciplinary hearings and collective bargaining.

🏥

Health & Wellness

Up to 35% off at Diagnofirm. No 10% patient share for members without medical aid.
