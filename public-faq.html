Frequently asked questions

Who can join BOPEU?
Any employee of the Government of Botswana or a parastatal where government has a financial interest. This includes central government, local authorities, land boards, and state-owned enterprises.
How much does membership cost?
Subscription is 1% of your basic salary, capped between P39.90 and P100.00 per month. It's deducted automatically via payroll stop-order.
How long until I can access benefits?
Most benefits are immediately available once your payroll deduction is active. The educational bursary requires 6 months of active membership.
What is the funeral cover worth?
P60,000 core cover for you, your spouse, and children under 21. Optional extended family cover available. Includes 12-month premium waiver if you pass away.
Can I add extended family to my cover?
Yes. Parents, in-laws, and adult children can be added at approximately P88/month each. Up to 8 extended family members per policy.
How do I file a funeral claim?
Through the app — Claims → Submit claim. Required documents: death certificate, deceased's Omang, your Omang, proof of relationship, bank confirmation. Claims must be filed within 6 months of death.
How do I apply for a bursary?
Through the app — Benefits → Educational Bursary → Apply. You'll need: admission letter, quote/invoice, payslip, and Omang. Up to P30,000 interest-free, repaid over up to 24 months.
What is the SACCOS loan process?
Emergency loans up to P20,000 with 48-hour approval. Ordinary loans up to P250,000 requiring 1:2 savings ratio. Apply through the app — Benefits → Babereki SACCOS.
Can I cancel my membership?
Yes — contact your local BOPEU office. Note that cancellation removes access to all benefits including funeral cover, loans, and bursary eligibility. Some benefits require notice periods.
What if I change employers?
Notify your new payroll office and BOPEU to update your stop-order. Your membership follows you between eligible employers. Contact info@bopeu.org.bw with your new employer details.
