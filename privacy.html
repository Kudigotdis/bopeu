Privacy Notice

Draft — subject to BOPEU review and legal sign-off. Last updated: 2026.

1. Who we are

The Botswana Public Employees Union ("BOPEU", "we", "us") operates the BOPEU Member Services Portal. BOPEU is the data controller for personal information submitted through the portal.

2. Information we collect

  • Identity: full names, Omang/ID number, date of birth, gender.
  • Contact: mobile, email, physical and postal address.
  • Employment: sector, employer, department, job title, salary scale, basic salary.
  • Membership: member number, office, subscriptions, benefits enrolled.
  • Applications, grievances and claims you submit through the portal, including attachments.
  • Technical: device type, browser, IP address, and audit trails of portal actions.

3. Why we collect it

  • To administer your membership and provide the services you request.
  • To process applications, benefits, grievances and insurance claims.
  • To send you union updates and targeted announcements relevant to your sector or office.
  • To meet legal, tax, audit and payroll obligations.
  • To keep the portal secure and detect misuse.

4. Lawful basis

We rely on your consent, our contract with you, our legal obligations, and our legitimate interests in administering the union — as provided by the Botswana Data Protection Act, 2024 (commenced 14 January 2025).

5. Who we share with

  • Benefit partners — only the minimum required to deliver the benefit. Partners see aggregated data only unless you expressly consent to personal information being shared.
  • Insurers and brokers (Babereki, Bona Life, BLIC, Metropolitan Life) — for insurance applications and claims.
  • Payroll and banking partners — for subscription deductions and loan repayments.
  • Service providers — hosting, email and SMS partners, under written data-processing agreements.
  • Regulators and law enforcement — where required by Botswana law.

6. How long we keep it

We keep membership records for the duration of your membership plus the retention period required by Botswana law. Application and claim records are kept for the period required by the relevant scheme and tax law. Audit logs are retained for a minimum of 24 months.

7. Your rights

  • Access a copy of the personal information we hold about you.
  • Ask us to correct inaccurate or incomplete information.
  • Ask us to delete information we no longer have a lawful reason to keep.
  • Object to or restrict certain processing.
  • Withdraw consent for marketing or non-essential processing at any time.
  • Complain to the Information and Data Protection Commissioner (Botswana).

8. Security

We use role-based access, encryption in transit, encryption at rest where required, audit logging, backups, and vendor due diligence. Staff access to member information is logged and reviewed.

9. Cross-border transfers

Some service providers may process data outside Botswana. Where this happens, we will rely on appropriate safeguards under the Botswana Data Protection Act, 2024.
