Privacy Policy

BOPEU Privacy Policy

We are committed to protecting your personal data. This application collects and stores member information necessary for:

  • Managing union membership
  • Processing funeral and insurance claims
  • Loan scheme administration (BOPEU Direct / Babereki / Access Bank)
  • Communication with members

Data Security: All data is encrypted and stored locally on your device first. WhatsApp integration uses standard HTTPS protocols.

Your Rights: You can request data deletion, correction, or access at any time by contacting your BOPEU office.

Contact: info@bopeu.org.bw | Tel: 267 3214 200
