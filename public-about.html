About BOPEU

The Botswana Public Employees Union (BOPEU) is a registered trade union representing public sector employees across central government, local authorities, land boards and parastatals.

Our history

Founded in 2007 (with roots in the BCSA from 1942), BOPEU has grown to over 37,000 members across Botswana.

Our mission

To protect, represent and empower public sector workers through collective bargaining, legal representation, and accessible benefits that improve the quality of life for our members and their families.

Our values

  • Justice — for every member, in every workplace
  • Equality — regardless of grade, gender or region
  • Representation — a strong, collective voice
  • Solidarity — standing together locally and internationally
  • Empowerment — informed, engaged members

Coverage

BOPEU covers five sectors:

  • Central Government (ministries and departments)
  • Local Authorities (city, town and district councils)
  • Land Boards
  • Parastatals and State-Owned Enterprises
  • Statutory bodies with government financial interest

Offices

Headquarters: Gaborone CBD, Grand Union Building, Plot 54374. Regional offices: Francistown, Palapye, Maun, Kang.
