What this agreement covers

1. Aggregated-only data rule — Partners receive only aggregated, anonymised statistics. No individual member data unless the member has given explicit consent for a specific transaction.

2. Consent-gated transactions — Individual data (e.g. phone for CUG, ID for eligibility) is accessible only after the member initiates the request in the BOPEU app.

3. Access logging — Every data access is logged with your user ID, timestamp, and the specific record accessed. BOPEU DPO reviews monthly.

4. Breach notification — You must notify BOPEU within 24 hours of any breach involving BOPEU member data.

5. Data retention — Your access to member data expires 90 days after the transaction closes. Offline copies are prohibited.

6. Botswana jurisdiction — Any disputes resolved under Botswana law.
