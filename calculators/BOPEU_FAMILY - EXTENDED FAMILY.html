  • Subscription fee is 1% of basic salary (capped P50.00 – P120.00).
  • Compulsory funeral cover of P61.70 for member and immediate family, with optional 12-month premium waiver at P66.40.
  • Benefit for member and spouse is P65,000.00. Children’s benefits illustrated in the scheme.
  • Limits: maximum of 6 children and 8 extended family members per policy.
  • Extended family must be under age 75 and can include dependents, brothers, sisters, uncles, nephews, nieces, grandparents, grandchildren, second spouse, daughters-in-law, brothers-in-law, sisters-in-law, cousins and aunts. In-laws only if legally married.
  • Adult child cover ceases at 35 years — the onus is on the parent to change cover to extended family cover.
  • Waiting periods: 3 months for main member and family; 6 months for parents (natural deaths); 12 months for extended family (natural deaths); 24 months for suicide. Immediate cover for accidental death once one month’s premium is paid.
  • Amendment waiting period: 12 months for any new benefit added.
  • Membership ceases if subscriptions are unpaid for 3 consecutive months. Reinstatement attracts waiting periods.
  • Cancellation requires 3 months’ written notice. No claims or refunds after cancellation.
  • Claims paid within 48 hours of receiving all requested documents. Notification within 6 months of death; all documents within 12 months.
  • Data protection: personal data processed under the Botswana Data Protection Act, 2018 (as amended 2024). KYC and transaction records retained for 20 years per the Financial Intelligence Act.

Rates as per the BOPEU Group Funeral Scheme & Extended Family Application Form.
Premiums in Botswana Pula (BWP). Underwritten by Babereki Life Insurance Company (BLIC).
