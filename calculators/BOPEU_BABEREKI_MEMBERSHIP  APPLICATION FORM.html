  • Subscription fee: 1% of basic salary, capped from P50.00 to P120.00.
  • Compulsory funeral cover: P61.70 for member and immediate family (spouse and children). Optional 12-month premium waiver at P66.40.
  • Benefit: member and spouse P65,000.00. Children’s benefits illustrated per age band.
  • Member: an employee of the Government of the Republic of Botswana or any organization where government has financial interest, who has not attained age 65 years. Policy includes compulsory accidental cover for main member and spouse.
  • Spouse: legally married, or a union recognized as marriage in accordance with Botswana law and customs, nominated as spouse under this policy.
  • Child: by birth, step, or adopted (including a child born out of wedlock) under 21 years, extended to 25 years if still in full study. Stillborn child (24+ weeks intra-uterine existence) covered. No age limit for physically or mentally handicapped children with proof of condition.
  • Adult child cover ceases at 35 years — the onus is on the parent to change cover to extended family cover.
  • Limits: maximum 6 children and 8 extended family members per policy.
  • Extended family: member must be within 5 years of attaining age 60 to nominate, and nominees must be under age 75.
  • Waiting periods: 1 calendar month for main member and family; 6 months for parents (natural deaths); 12 months for extended family (natural deaths); 24 months for suicide. Immediate cover for accidental death once one month’s premium is paid. Following amendment, 6-month waiting period for new benefit.
  • Death benefit ceases upon termination of membership in writing, failure to pay premiums for 3 consecutive months, or the day the main member dies.
  • Claims: paid within 48 hours of receiving all requested documents. Notification within 6 months of death; all documents within 12 months.
  • Claims submitted at: BOPEU branches in Francistown, Kang, Palapye and Maun. For Gaborone and surrounding areas, at Babereki Insurance Brokers Head Office.
  • Participation in the Group Life Assurance scheme is mandatory for all members.
  • Scheme underwritten by Bona Life Insurance.
