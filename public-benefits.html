Your benefits as a BOPEU member

Financial, family, health, lifestyle and legal support — all under one membership.

🎓

Educational Bursary

Interest-free assistance up to P30,000 for tuition, accommodation, books and research. Repay over 24 months. Requires 6+ months of active membership.

🏠

Building Materials

Archein Hardware scheme — up to P200,000 credit, 4% monthly interest on reducing balance, up to 36 months, no deposit.

💰

Emergency Loans

P2,001 – P20,000 through Babereki SACCOS with 48-hour turnaround. Also Quick Loans (P500 – P5,000) and Ordinary Loans (up to P250,000).

🛡️

Funeral Cover

Group cover underwritten by Metropolitan Life. P60,000 core for member + spouse + children under 21. 1% of basic salary (capped P39.90 – P100.00). 12-month premium waiver.

🏥

Diagnofirm Health

Up to 35% off lab tests for members without medical aid. No 10% patient share. Virgin Active 10% off, Penalty Box 15% off.

⚖️

Legal Representation

Free legal support on workplace disputes, disciplinary hearings, collective bargaining and labour matters.

🛍️

Lifestyle Discounts

Up to 10% off at selected supermarkets, hotels, travel, hardware and automotive partners.

💳

Financial Services

Access Bank partnership — personal loans up to P700,000, mortgages up to P3,000,000. First Capital Bank facility up to P200,000.

📱

Telecom Partnerships

Discounted airtime and data through Mascom and Orange. Mobile money benefits for members.
