Documents Required to Process a Claim
  • Claim form, which will be completed at Babereki Life Insurance Company Head Office.
  • Certified death certificate and certified birth certificate for minors claim.
  • A certified copy of the claimant’s identity (Omang) and proof of age is required before any benefit is paid.
  • Marriage certificate for spouse’s claim where applicable.
  • Police report for accidental death occurring before waiting period elapses, or for suicide that happens after suicide waiting period has elapsed.
  • Proof of relationship where surname differs.
  • An affidavit where necessary.
  • Completed Babereki Life Insurance Company KYC Form for claimant including proof of residency.
  • Proof of Bank Account for claimant.
  • Confirmation of employment letter or Affidavit.
  • Recent payslip.

Further information or more documents with proof of deceased’s relation to member may also be requested by Babereki Life Insurance Company.

Upon receipt of all documentation requested, claim will be paid within 48 hours. Death Claim notification or death claim documents must be submitted within six months from time of death and no claim or refund shall be admitted by Babereki Life Insurance Company unless a written notice was received within 6 (six) months. All claims documents should be submitted within 12 months of the date of death.

  • 3 month waiting period for the main member and his/her family.
  • There will be a waiting period of 6 months for parents and twelve (12) months for extended family covers on natural deaths. There is however immediate cover for accidental death as long as one month premium has been paid.
  • 24 Months for suicide deaths.
  • Following amendment to new benefit there will be a twelve (12) month waiting period for the new benefit.

Death benefit and refund of any kind shall cease on the day that:

  • Upon termination of membership by member in writing.
  • Failure to pay premiums for three (3) consecutive months.
  • The day when main Member dies.
